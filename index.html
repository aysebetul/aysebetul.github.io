I am an AI researcher and PhD candidate at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg. My research focuses on EEG foundation models, deep learning, brain-computer interfaces, and brain decoding.

I am particularly interested in learning meaningful representations from neural signals and developing reliable AI methods for understanding human mental states.

Research Interests

  • EEG foundation models
  • Brain decoding and brain-computer interfaces
  • Deep learning for neural time series
  • Self-supervised and representation learning
  • Explainable and human-centred AI
