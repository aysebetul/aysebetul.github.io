I am a PhD candidate and AI researcher at AILab, Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg. My research focuses on deep learning and foundation models for time-series data, with EEG as my primary application domain.

Within this area, I explore self-supervised learning, representation learning, and transformer-based models for complex temporal data. My current work examines how pretrained models learn transferable EEG representations and how architectural and training choices affect performance across datasets and downstream tasks. More broadly, I aim to develop robust and adaptable deep learning methods for time-series analysis and human-centred applications.

Research Areas: Time-Series Deep Learning · Foundation Models · Self-Supervised Learning · Representation Learning · EEG Analysis · Human-Centred AI · Affective Computing
